# Agent Time 0.2.0-beta.57

Review shows the selected day before checking the previous seven days in the background. Historical checks yield between days, reuse existing caches and cannot replace a more recent day or decision with a late response. Loading the day and checking other days have separate, truthful feedback.

Human visits, agent executions and saved billing reports share a details panel. Recorded intervals remain separate, including their gaps; measured agent work stays distinct from declared task timers. Escape closes details and restores the originating control. Retained pages keep their context without leaving menus on another page.

Home human activity rows, including website parents and pages, offer project assignment and undo using the same recorded pairs and split segments as Review. Drag-and-drop and Home-to-Review navigation preserve the exact original selection even when a larger group appears after a refresh. Time and Billing can open a day in Review without directly assigning a multi-day aggregate or changing a saved snapshot.

Time, Billing and Usage display their scope explicitly: full period or day, repository or complete billable project, live activity or saved snapshot. Saved Billing dates, totals and exports retain their original scope when global filters change. Missing coverage is distinguished from observed zero; exclusion causes are not invented.

Timing/counting formulas, original observations, manual decisions, billing policies and saved reports are unchanged. Equality and preservation checks passed on an isolated 646 MiB history. Review's useful first response improved on that fixture; cold 30-day reports remain expensive. No productivity score or additional content collection is introduced.
